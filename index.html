Research

Momentum in Indian Equities, 1997–2026

SSRN submission planned

Student paper · Manipal University Jaipur

Does buying last year's winners work on the NSE? I tested it with point-in-time data, so no survivorship bias. Classic 12-1 momentum returned about 23% a year after brokerage fees and tax, but had deep crashes. Simple trend filters cut the crash drawdown roughly in half. A low-drawdown variant (the top 10 Nifty 200 stocks near their 52-week high) returned 24.4% a year with a maximum drawdown of −28%.
